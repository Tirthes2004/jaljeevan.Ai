On-spot assessment: RTRWH & Artificial Recharge

A practical web/mobile tool to estimate rooftop rainwater harvesting potential, recommend recharge structures, and provide sizing & cost guidance.

Core deliverables — quick, actionable outputs for citizens and field teams.

  • Feasibility check

    Instant assessment using roof area, rainfall input and runoff coefficient to tell if RTRWH is viable.

    Runs a location-aware algorithm combining roof geometry and local rainfall to estimate how much rain can be captured annually and whether infiltration is feasible on-site.

  • Suggested structure types

    Recommends pit, trench, shaft or soakaway types based on site constraints.

    Chooses the most suitable RTRWH/AR structure considering available open space, soil permeability, and expected runoff volumes.

  • Hydrogeology snapshot

    Principal aquifer type and depth-to-water at the location.

    Presents nearby aquifer characteristics and recent groundwater level ranges so users understand recharge potential and safety margins.

  • Local rainfall & seasonal profile

    Monthly rainfall patterns used to size the system.

    Uses local (station or gridded) rainfall to create a monthly capture profile and recommend storage/recharge timing.

  • Runoff & capture estimates

    Estimated annual harvest and peak-runoff rates.

    Presents expected harvest volume (m³/yr) and peak runoff for design storms so sizing is robust under extremes.

  • Recommended dimensions

    Suggested sizes for recharge pits, trenches, shafts or percolation systems.

    Gives quick-build dimensions with safety buffers and expected infiltration rates based on simple geotechnical inputs.

  • Cost & benefit estimate

    Ballpark cost and simple payback / benefit analysis.

    Produces an order-of-magnitude cost estimate and a simple lifecycle cost-benefit to help users decide investment levels.

  • Regional language support

    Form and outputs prepared for easy translation and offline printing.

    Design is modular to add Marathi/Hindi/Bengali/Tamil/Telugu strings — critical for adoption in rural & urban communities.

  • Community & field workflow

    Generates printable guidance & field-checklists for installation teams.

    Produces a short installation checklist (material list, safety notes) and a shareable report for authorities or homeowners.
