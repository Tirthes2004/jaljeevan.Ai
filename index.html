System Architecture

End-to-end pipeline from user inputs to AI+GIS processing, AR visualization, cost-benefit, and community gamification.

  • Data Layer: Rainfall (IMD), Groundwater (CGWB), aquifer & soil.
  • Processing: AI calculator, runoff estimation, cost-benefit, structure recommender.
  • Visualization: 3D/AR overlays of pits, trenches, and tanks.
  • Gamification: Credits, leaderboards, challenges, social sharing.
  • Marketplace: Vendors, subsidies, RTRWH kits.
